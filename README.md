A voicemail greeting recorded by a cat, in one line.
`main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You've reached the cat. I heard it ring, I chose the warm laptop.
> Leave a message; I'll sit on that too.

The cat heard the phone and chose the warm laptop anyway — that
deliberate snub is the character. The joke is specific to cats
(sitting on warm laptops, and on anything you leave them), and the
punch — "I'll sit on that too" — lands in the last words, spoken
aloud in the few seconds a caller waits.

Revision note: the first draft had the cat merely asleep on the
laptop. The revision gives the cat a choice and an attitude, which
is what makes it a character speaking rather than a description.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (104 characters, 1 line)
```
